Physics settings

Inverse dynamics Acceleration → required effort

Zero acceleration gives a static-load calculation only when the mechanism is at rest.
Six internal spin accelerations are set to zero. This is a required-effort calculation, not an executed controller or predicted motor current. Contact and stop forces are excluded. Underactuation is an allocation residual.
Physics model boundaries

The platform, attached payload, six barrels and six rods have mass and inertia. Internal leg spin is integrated, so active and passive spherical-joint torques affect the dynamics.

Stroke stops and the floor use compliant penalty contact. Classic experiments use ideal effort sources. Ball Lab optionally adds a 24 V averaged drive, drive friction and ball/deck contact. Energy shown here is mechanism-only, not ball/electrical total energy. General self-collision, backlash, flexible links, thermal electronics and hardware safety certification are excluded.

STEWART / QUICK START

Forces make the motion.

The lab has two uses: keep a ball on a moving plate, or inspect the platform and control it directly. Opening this help pauses the simulation.

Play with the ball

Use Ball Lab at the top of the page. Click the small top view to move a target, choose Trace a circle, or Push the ball. The orange ball is simulated; blue/purple markers are measured/predicted positions, not camera pixels. Press Run if paused.

Take over manually, then return

Tilt the plate manually gives the pose sliders control while keeping the ball and drive. Manual effort in Control instead commands individual active joints; it starts paused because position holding is off. Use Return to ball control or the Ball Lab tab to resume automatic feedback. A fallen ball is reset by that action; a numerical stop requires Reset tested setup.

Reset and recover

Reset ball & balance preserves drive/sensor parameters and clears the ball, path, observer and history. Reset tested setup (or Reset lab in the scene) restores the tested 24 V Ball Lab preset. In the platform workbench, Reset preserves design geometry. Classic preset buttons start a fresh ideal experiment. Save project first.

Explore the workbench

Use IK / pose control for platform targets, Compliance for a virtual spring, Gravity for weight support without position feedback, or Drives off for zero commanded effort. Live current can decay in the finite servo profile. The same Kp/Kd fields do not configure both ideal motors and the finite servo: inapplicable controls are disabled and explained.

Save and measure

Save project / Load project round-trip the experiment and open imports paused. Platform CSV contains mechanism states and efforts; Ball CSV contains ball targets, measurements and drive readouts. Export geometry only is not a resumable project. The page does not send your simulation data to a server.

Limits

This is an educational custom rigid-body model, not hardware validation, a manufactured CAD assembly or a general-purpose collision engine. The optional drive and sensors use declared generic assumptions. The full equations, sources and model boundaries are in Learn. On a slow device the simulation may run below real time; the displayed real-time factor reports that separately from control behavior.
