Physics settings

Inverse dynamics Acceleration → required effort

Zero acceleration gives a static-load calculation only when the mechanism is at rest.
Six internal spin accelerations are set to zero. Contact and stop forces are excluded. Underactuation is reported as an allocation residual.
Physics model boundaries

The platform, attached payload, six barrels and six rods have mass and inertia. Internal leg spin is integrated, so active and passive spherical-joint torques affect the dynamics.

Stroke stops and the floor use compliant penalty contact. Leg collisions, friction, backlash, flexible links and motor electrical dynamics are excluded. This is not manufacturing or hardware safety certification.

STEWART / QUICK START

Forces make the motion.

By default, six linear motors are active and twelve ball joints are passive. IK converts the target pose into leg lengths. PD force control moves the actual platform.

1. Move the platform

Change X/Y/Z or Roll/Pitch/Yaw in Control to stop the automatic trajectory and track your target. Active and passive states are also written in the joint table; the dashed outline is the target. Tracking errors are never hidden by snapping the mechanism into place.

2. Try a virtual spring

Click Push with 16 N. With K=800 N/m the default equilibrium displacement is about 20 mm. In Dynamics, click Release all forces to watch it return. The separate Push button applies a 2 N·s impulse, not a sustained force.

3. Explore passive joints

Select a leg or end joint in the scene, or use the B/P/T table. Passive disables its motor and uses k/c springs and dampers. k=c=0 means free, not locked. Capture rest stores the current length or orientation as the spring's unstrained reference.

4. Explore kinematics and dynamics

The IK · FK tab independently solves a pose from six lengths. This local solution depends on the home or previous seed; it does not enumerate all assembly modes. Dynamics exposes external wrench, gravity, timestep and inverse-dynamics acceleration inputs.

5. Save an experiment

Project JSON stores geometry, current state, target, controller settings and all eighteen joint groups. CSV exports sampled states, lengths, efforts and settings. The standalone simulator works offline and sends no simulation data to a server.

What this model does not prove

This is a custom reduced-coordinate 6-SPS model with twelve generalized speeds and fourteen moving rigid bodies (thirteen without payload). It is not a MuJoCo or industrial-solver execution. Kinematic closure is enforced by construction. Mass, inertia, gravity, velocity coupling and joint forces are included. Ball-joint k/c is isotropic. Arbitrary topology, self-collision, friction, backlash, flexibility and hardware safety are outside scope. See the source tests and validation notes before making engineering claims.
